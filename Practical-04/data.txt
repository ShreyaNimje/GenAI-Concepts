# Ria and the Magic Red Balloon

Once upon a time, there was a little rabbit named Ria. Ria was a small, soft, white rabbit with long ears and a tiny pink nose. She lived with her mother in a cozy little house near a big green forest.

Ria loved playing outside. Every morning, she would wake up early, brush her teeth, eat her breakfast, and run outside to play.

One bright and sunny morning, Ria went for a walk in the forest. She saw colorful flowers, green trees, and little birds singing sweet songs.

“Tweet, tweet! Good morning, Ria!” sang the birds.

“Good morning!” Ria smiled and waved.

As Ria walked along the forest path, she saw something under a big tree.

“What is that?” she wondered.

She hopped closer and found a beautiful **red balloon** lying on the grass.

“Wow!” said Ria. “It is so pretty!”

Ria picked up the balloon. It was bright red and shiny. She held the string tightly and danced around happily.

“I have a new balloon!” she shouted.

Ria decided to take the balloon home and show it to her mother.

She started walking home. But suddenly, a strong wind began to blow.

“Whoosh! Whoosh!”

The leaves danced in the air. Ria held the balloon tightly.

“Oh! The wind is very strong,” she said.

But then the string slipped from her little paw.

“Oops!”

The red balloon flew into the sky.

“Oh no!” cried Ria. “My balloon!”

The balloon went higher and higher.

Ria ran after it.

“Come back! Come back!” she called.

But the balloon did not stop.

Ria became very sad. She sat under a tree and began to cry.

Just then, a little yellow bird flew down and sat beside her.

“Why are you crying, Ria?” asked the bird.

“My red balloon flew away,” said Ria sadly. “I really liked it.”

The little bird looked up at the sky.

“Do not worry, Ria. I will help you,” said the bird.

The bird flew up into the air. It flew higher and higher.

Ria watched with hope.

The bird saw the red balloon floating near a tall tree.

“Got you!” said the bird.

It carefully caught the balloon string with its tiny beak.

But the balloon was very high, and the wind was strong.

The bird flew slowly back toward Ria.

“Be careful!” shouted Ria.

After a few moments, the bird came back and dropped the balloon into Ria’s paws.

“My balloon!” Ria shouted happily.

She hugged the balloon.

“Thank you so much!” she said.

“You are welcome,” said the little bird.

Ria smiled.

“You are very kind. What is your name?”

“My name is Sunny,” said the bird.

“Sunny, would you like to be my friend?”

“Yes!” said Sunny happily.

From that day, Ria and Sunny became the best of friends.

Every morning, they played together in the forest. Ria hopped between the flowers while Sunny flew above her.

They played hide-and-seek.

Ria hid behind a big tree.

“Where is Ria?” Sunny asked.

“I am here!” Ria laughed.

Sometimes, they sat beside the little pond and watched the fish swim.

One afternoon, Ria and Sunny were walking near the pond when they heard a tiny sound.

“Help! Help!”

They looked around.

Under a bush, they saw a small baby squirrel.

“What happened?” asked Ria.

“I cannot find my mother,” cried the squirrel.

“Do not worry,” said Ria. “We will help you.”

Ria and Sunny looked around the forest. Sunny flew up into the trees while Ria searched under the bushes.

After some time, Sunny heard a squirrel calling.

“Chik-chik! Chik-chik!”

“I found her!” Sunny shouted.

The baby squirrel ran to its mother.

“Mommy!”

The mother squirrel hugged her baby.

“Thank you, Ria and Sunny,” she said.

Ria smiled.

“We are happy to help.”

As the sun began to set, Ria looked at her red balloon.

She thought about the day she found it.

She remembered how sad she had felt when it flew away. She also remembered how Sunny had helped her.

Ria understood something very important.

A balloon could make her happy, but a good friend could make her heart happy.

Ria hugged Sunny.

“I am very lucky to have you as my friend,” she said.

Sunny smiled.

“And I am lucky to have you too!”

The two friends watched the orange sun go down behind the trees.

Ria then went home with her red balloon.

Her mother was waiting at the door.

“Where were you, Ria?” asked her mother.

“I had a wonderful day!” said Ria.

She told her mother about the balloon, Sunny, and the little squirrel.

Her mother smiled and said, “That is wonderful, Ria. Always remember to help others and be kind.”

“I will, Mommy,” said Ria.

That night, Ria placed her red balloon beside her bed.

She closed her eyes and smiled.

She was happy because she had a beautiful balloon, a kind heart, and most importantly, a wonderful friend.

And from that day on, Ria and Sunny helped everyone they met in the forest.

They learned that **kindness, friendship, and helping others make every day special.**

**Moral: A true friend is always ready to help. Be kind and help others.**
